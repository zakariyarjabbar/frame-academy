# Color & Mood — practice worksheet

FRAME ACADEMY · Original portfolio lesson

## Intention
Explain and apply a simple color treatment consistently.

## Before you begin
Comfort opening and adjusting images in an editor. No specialist color-grading software required.

- Three related photographs or clips
- An editor with exposure, white balance and saturation
- A display in reasonably consistent room light

## Your brief
Choose three related images. Correct obvious exposure and white-balance mismatches, then apply a simple treatment with one dominant color relationship. Compare the set and write a repeatable rule for the look.

## Check your work
- [ ] I corrected the three starting images.
- [ ] I applied one treatment rule across the set.
- [ ] I compared neutrals, important colors and shadow detail.

## Reflection
What is your treatment rule, and which image needed a different correction to fit the set?
