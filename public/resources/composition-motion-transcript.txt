Composition in Motion — FRAME ACADEMY
Silent visual teaching clip; equivalent text.

[0s] Give movement somewhere to go
The cyclist is small but clear. A quiet background and space ahead let movement organize the frame.
Subject + direction + space

[10s] Separate the subject
Check contrast against the background. A busy shape behind a face or wheel can hide the action even when your subject is centered.
Look at the silhouette

[20s] Use lead room
Leave room in front of the moving subject. Compare a balanced position with a deliberately tight one and notice the different tension.
Space ahead suggests a destination

[30s] Keep screen direction
Rightward motion followed by rightward motion connects easily. An unexplained reversal can suggest the subject turned back.
Shot A → Shot B →

[40s] Change scale for a reason
Wide: where are we? Medium: what is happening? Detail: what matters now? Each cut should give useful information.
WIDE / MEDIUM / DETAIL

[50s] Film one action
Make three views. Check the edges and direction. Watch without sound and describe the action in one clear sentence.
Three views. One readable action.
