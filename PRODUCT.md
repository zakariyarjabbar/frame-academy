# FRAME ACADEMY
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-required Next.js App Router, TypeScript strict mode, static export. npm for this new empty project. Browser-only learner data; no database or service keys.

## Users and purpose
An agency portfolio academy for aspiring photographers, filmmakers, designers and creators, beginner to early professional. Visitors discover a technique, watch a concise demonstration, make a study, and reflect on choices. Prospective clients can inspect a working learning product in minutes.

## Brand commitments
FRAME ACADEMY. Learn to see differently. Photography, film, and visual storytelling. Approved cinematic direction A. Confident, direct, creative, serious about craft. Fictional self-initiated portfolio identity; no measured outcomes, accreditation, awards or endorsements.

## Catalog
Light & Shadow ($49, beginner); Composition in Motion ($59, beginner); Portraits with Presence ($69, intermediate); The Art of the Short Film ($79, beginner); Color & Mood ($59, intermediate); Edit for the Story ($69, intermediate). USD is fictional catalog configuration. Each has one 45–120 second video, one 350–650 word illustrated reading, one practice assignment, a worksheet and a five-question quiz. Fictional instructors: Mara Ellis (light/portraits), Elias Noor (composition/short film), June Park (color/editing).

## Journeys and routes
Discover at `/`, `/courses`, `/courses/[slug]`, `/instructors`, `/instructors/[slug]`, `/about`. Review at `/enroll?course=...`; receipt at `/enrollment/confirmation?ref=...`. Learn through eighteen `/learn/[courseSlug]/[lessonSlug]` pages and six `/learn/[courseSlug]/quiz` pages. Personal workspace: `/my-learning`, `/saved`, `/notes`, `/certificates`, `/certificate?course=...`, `/profile`. Help: `/help`, `/contact`, `/demo`, `/privacy`, missing-page states.

## Completion rules
Video ends or is manually marked; reading is explicitly marked; practice requires all checklist items and a saved reflection. Quiz passes at 4/5. All three lessons plus a passed quiz issue one certificate. Later failed attempts preserve a prior pass. Continue returns to the last unfinished lesson or remaining quiz. Issued certificates retain their name snapshot.

## Persistence and scope
Current browser and origin only; plain-text local records, no synchronization. Duplicate enrollment reuses access. Resets require confirmation and touch only this app. Support requests are saved locally and receive no live reply. Enrollments collect no payment or credentials. All static content is inspectable. No CMS, analytics, community, uploads, protected media or offline video promise.

## Acceptance
Every required route, lesson, resource, quiz and local workflow must work. Accurate media duration, social covers with deterministic typography, static HTML metadata, responsive keyboard-accessible interfaces, actual tests and visual QA. Public deployment is not authorized; public origin remains an explicit build-time setting.
