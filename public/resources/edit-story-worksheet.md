# Edit for the Story — practice worksheet

FRAME ACADEMY · Original portfolio lesson

## Intention
Assemble a short sequence with intentional rhythm and continuity.

## Before you begin
Know how to import, trim and arrange clips. Your own three-shot film can supply the material.

- Three or more clips of a simple action
- An editor with trim and reorder controls
- Headphones if your clips include useful sound

## Your brief
Create two short edits from the same clips: an observational version and a tighter version. Preserve understandable actions in both. Compare them at normal playback speed and choose one based on your intended experience.

## Check your work
- [ ] I selected clips by the information they contribute.
- [ ] I checked action, prop and screen-direction continuity.
- [ ] I compared two rhythms at normal speed.

## Reflection
Which timing choice changed the experience most, and why did you choose your final version?
