Portraits with Presence — FRAME ACADEMY
Silent visual teaching clip; equivalent text.

[0s] A person, not a pose
Begin with a shared idea and a willing subject. Light, background and direction should support that intention.
Agree on the intention

[10s] Quiet the background
Check bright patches and lines behind the head. Move the camera or subject until the face has a clear place in the frame.
Check the edges first

[20s] Read the face in light
The lit cheek and gradual shadow describe the face. Look for detail on both sides before choosing your exposure.
Light describes; shadow shapes

[30s] Give one clear direction
“Look just past my shoulder” is something a person can do. Make one request, pause, and observe the result.
One instruction. Then a pause.

[40s] Compare the eye line
A direct gaze addresses the viewer. A gaze out of frame points attention elsewhere. Choose the relationship your portrait needs.
Toward the viewer / beyond the frame

[50s] Make a portrait pair
Keep light and background. Change one direction. Compare the frames and explain which one matches the agreed idea.
Two portraits. One deliberate change.
