# Composition in Motion — practice worksheet

FRAME ACADEMY · Original portfolio lesson

## Intention
Frame a short visual sequence with clear subject emphasis.

## Before you begin
No filmmaking experience needed. You should be able to record and review a short clip.

- A phone or camera
- A safe place to film a simple action
- A basic editor or three still frames

## Your brief
Record or photograph one simple action in wide, medium and close views. Keep its screen direction consistent. Assemble the views in order or compare them side by side. Do not film in traffic or direct a person into an unsafe position.

## Check your work
- [ ] I identified a single subject and action.
- [ ] I made three views with purposeful scale changes.
- [ ] I checked screen direction and distracting edges.

## Reflection
What new information does each frame add, and where does your eye go first?
