The Art of the Short Film — FRAME ACADEMY
Silent visual teaching clip; equivalent text.

[0s] A story in three shots
A place. An action. A consequence. A small visible change can carry a complete visual beat.
PLACE → ACTION → CONSEQUENCE

[10s] Write what can be filmed
“Feels uncertain” is internal. “Opens a letter, pauses, puts it away” gives the camera observable actions.
Use visible verbs

[20s] Establish the place
The diner window locates our reader. A wide view gives the closer action a context.
Shot 1: where are we?

[30s] Reveal the action
Move closer to the letter. Keep hand, prop and screen direction consistent when repeating the movement.
Shot 2: what changes?

[40s] Show a consequence
A letter kept or left behind means something different. Let the final image register the decision.
Shot 3: what remains?

[50s] Make a small turning point
Sketch three views, film them, and watch without music. Can another viewer describe the visible change?
One location. One prop. Three shots.
