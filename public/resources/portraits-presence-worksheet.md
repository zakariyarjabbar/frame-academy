# Portraits with Presence — practice worksheet

FRAME ACADEMY · Original portfolio lesson

## Intention
Plan a portrait through light, background, and direction.

## Before you begin
Comfort with basic exposure and focus. Light & Shadow is a useful starting point.

- A phone or camera
- Window light and a quiet background
- A willing adult subject, or yourself

## Your brief
Make two portraits with a willing adult subject or yourself. Establish the same light and background, then vary only the eye line or shoulder angle. Explain your choice without making judgments about the person’s appearance.

## Check your work
- [ ] I agreed on the idea and image use with my subject, or used a self-portrait.
- [ ] I checked the background and light before directing.
- [ ] I made two frames with one deliberate change.

## Reflection
Which change best supported the portrait’s intention, and how did you communicate it?
