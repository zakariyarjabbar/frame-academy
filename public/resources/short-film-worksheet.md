# The Art of the Short Film — practice worksheet

FRAME ACADEMY · Original portfolio lesson

## Intention
Turn a small idea into a coherent three-shot sequence.

## Before you begin
No screenwriting background required. Choose an action you can safely stage in a familiar location.

- A phone or camera
- One accessible location and a simple prop
- Any editor that can trim and join clips

## Your brief
Write a one-sentence idea, sketch three shots, and record a 10–30 second sequence around a simple prop. Use the last shot to show a consequence. Keep the footage on your device and submit the three-shot logic as a reflection.

## Check your work
- [ ] I wrote an idea with a visible change.
- [ ] I made establishing, action and consequence shots.
- [ ] I checked the sequence without music or explanatory titles.

## Reflection
Describe the three shots. What does the final image change about the opening?
