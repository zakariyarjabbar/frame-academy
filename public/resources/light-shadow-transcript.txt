Light & Shadow — FRAME ACADEMY
Silent visual teaching clip; equivalent text.

[0s] One light, three decisions
Watch the sphere, not the lamp. Direction, apparent size and reflected light each change how a simple shape reads.
Observe the light study

[10s] Find the direction
The bright left face and shadow stretching right tell us the light arrives from the upper left. Read the shadow before moving anything.
Light → subject → shadow

[20s] Compare the edge
A narrow transition feels crisp. A broad transition feels soft. Softness depends on the apparent size of the source from the subject.
Small source / crisp edge • large source / soft edge

[30s] Move to the side
Front light flattens visible shading. Side light reveals the sphere’s volume and the texture in the linen. Keep exposure locked while comparing.
Front light → side light

[40s] Add a little fill
A white card on the shadow side reflects light back. Bring it closer to reveal more detail; move it away to keep a deeper shadow.
White card lifts the shadow

[50s] Make your own study
Photograph front light, side light and side light with fill. Choose one image. Describe the decision that made the shape read clearly.
Make 3 frames. Compare. Choose.
