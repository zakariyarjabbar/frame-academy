Color & Mood — FRAME ACADEMY
Silent visual teaching clip; equivalent text.

[0s] Build a color relationship
Blue leads the field. Rust supports the warm region. A small orange accent gives the eye somewhere to land.
DOMINANT / SUPPORT / ACCENT

[10s] Describe before adjusting
Name the important color roles and their relative area. Decide whether the accent points to your subject.
Area changes the role of a color

[20s] Correct the starting point
Compare exposure and white balance first. One preset cannot automatically repair three different starting conditions.
CORRECTION → TREATMENT

[30s] Write a simple rule
Try cool shadows, restrained saturation and preserved warm highlights. Small linked decisions are easier to repeat than random sliders.
A rule you can explain

[40s] Compare the set
Match the visual relationships, not just the numbers. Check familiar colors and keep useful shadow detail.
Same intention ≠ identical settings

[50s] Make three images belong
Save a before-and-after pair. Write the treatment rule and explain which correction differed across your three images.
Correct. Treat. Compare.
