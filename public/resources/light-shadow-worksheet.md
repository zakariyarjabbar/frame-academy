# Light & Shadow — practice worksheet

FRAME ACADEMY · Original portfolio lesson

## Intention
Use a single light source to shape a portrait or still life.

## Before you begin
No studio experience needed. Know how to take a photo and lock exposure on your camera or phone.

- A phone or camera with exposure lock
- One window or cool-running LED light
- A pale object, white card and dark cloth

## Your brief
Make three photographs of the same still life: front light, side light, and side light with white-card fill. Keep the camera position and exposure consistent wherever possible. Your photographs stay on your device; submit a short written observation here.

## Check your work
- [ ] I made all three lighting studies.
- [ ] I compared the shadow direction and edge.
- [ ] I chose one frame and can explain its lighting.

## Reflection
Which position described the shape best, and what changed when you added fill?
